The pending cleanup ticket owns the attempt/request binding and accepts only the exact event and lease echoed with Applied or AlreadyApplied. Verified warm reuse may advance calibration generation during its load handshake: the adapter must handle that owner-validated transition explicitly, not weaken equality or infer residency from `is_current()`.
